## 🌍 My Journey  

I’m **Nicholas**, a **Computer Science (B.Sc.)** and **Data Science (M.Sc.)** graduate.  
My fascination with technology started with programming — first with **Java** and **Python**, then expanding into **databases** and **software engineering**.  

Over time, I realized that **data is more than numbers**: it’s a canvas for stories, patterns, and intelligence.  
This passion led me into the world of **Machine Learning**, **Deep Learning**, and **AI**, where I love designing solutions that are both **impactful** and **explainable**.  

---

## 🔥 What I Work On  

- 📊 **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** → uncovering insights & patterns  
- 🧹 **Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering** → making data usable  
- 🤖 **Machine Learning Models** → regressions & predictive analysis  
- 🧠 **Deep Learning (CNNs)** → computer vision & representation learning  
- 🗣️ **LLMs** → fine-tuning & domain-specific adaptation  
- 🗄️ **Databases** → SQL (relational) & Cypher (graph-based)
